Our Story

Where Scent Becomes Reflection

LUMEN MOE was born from a desire to bring calm and meaning into everyday rituals. Each candle transforms scent into a quiet language of reflection an invitation to slow down, soften, and turn inward.

Rooted in honesty, kindness, and thoughtful craftsmanship, every piece blends fine fragrance with emotional clarity, shaping an atmosphere where light, scent, and self-awareness exist in gentle harmony.

About Us

LUMEN MOE is a collection of handcrafted home fragrances inspired by quiet living and timeless scents. All candles are non-toxic, paraffin-free, phthalate-free, cruelty-free, and plant based of coconut wax and soy wax.
